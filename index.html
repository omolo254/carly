    How ordering works

    Four easy steps

    1. Fill your bag

      Choose your scrunchies and pick a size for each one.

    2. Send your order

      Your order is written out for you. Send it to us by email.

    3. Get a Tikkie

      A parent checks the order and replies within two days with a Tikkie link to pay.

    4. Pick up or post

      Collect for free in Amsterdam, or we post it to you in the Netherlands for €2,50.

    About the maker

    Hi, I make every single one myself.

    I started sewing scrunchies for my friends and now I can't stop. I pick the fabrics, draw the designs and sew them on my sewing machine. Next I'm making bows, and after that sparkly bedazzled pieces with gems I set by hand.

    The money I earn goes into my savings and into more fabric, so every order helps me make the next design.
