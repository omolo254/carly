How ordering works

Four easy steps

  1. Fill your bag

    Pick your scrunchies and choose a size.

  2. Send your order

    We write your order out for you. Ask a grown-up to email it to us.

  3. Get a Tikkie

    We reply within two days with a Tikkie link, so a grown-up can pay.

  4. Pick up or post

    Pick it up for free in Amsterdam, or we post it to you in the Netherlands.
